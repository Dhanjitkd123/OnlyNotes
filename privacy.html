Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google or create an account, we collect basic profile information such as your email address and authentication tokens. Once registered, you may provide additional information such as your username, college, course details, and profile picture.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide and maintain the OnlyNotes marketplace. Your email is used for account security and login verification. Your username, avatar, and academic details are displayed on the platform to facilitate community interaction, purchasing, and peer-to-peer services.

3. Third-Party Services

OnlyNotes uses Supabase for database management, authentication, and file storage. We also use Google OAuth for secure login. These providers handle your data securely and in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local browser storage to save your preferences, such as your selected theme (Dark/Light mode) and session tokens to keep you logged securely into your account.
